Rub-a-Dub Blues: New Album Out November 20th

Gringo's TeamOctober 26, 2026Album ReleasePress

Recorded live to tape in a disused cork factory, Gringo's Washboard Band's third album brings twelve songs of hokum, ragtime and hot swing — and a European tour to go with it.

LISBON — October 26, 2026 — Gringo's Washboard Band today announce Rub-a-Dub Blues, their third studio album, out November 20th on Tábua Records. Produced by the band and engineered by long-time collaborator Paulo Fita, the record was captured in four days with two vintage ribbon microphones, no headphones and no overdubs.

The album features eight new originals alongside four reworked standards from the jug-band and hokum repertoire. The first single, "Scrub Me Mama", is out now on all streaming platforms, with an accompanying video shot in a working laundromat in Lisbon's Arroios neighbourhood.

The band describe the record as their most honest yet. "Everything you hear happened in the room at the same time," says washboard player Zé "Gringo" Tábuas. "If someone laughed, it's on the record. If the bass fell over, it's on the record."

"We wanted it to sound like the best Saturday night of your life — slightly out of tune, very loud, and over far too quickly."— Zé "Gringo" Tábuas, washboard & vocals
"I grew up on my grandfather's 78s. This is the first time I feel like we got close to that sound."— Rita Banjo, banjo & vocals
"The washtub bass is now officially held together with gaffer tape and love."— Nuno Tina, washtub bass

To celebrate the release, the band will embark on the Rub-a-Dub Tour 2026, with twelve dates across Portugal, Spain, France, the Netherlands and the UK, ending with a New Year's swing cruise from Lisbon to Funchal. Tickets are on sale now.

About Gringo's Washboard Band

Formed on a Lisbon street corner in 2016, Gringo's Washboard Band are a five-piece playing washboard, banjo, guitar, washtub bass, kazoo and harmonica. Drawing on the jug bands of Memphis, 1920s Chicago hokum and Parisian hot swing, they have played hundreds of shows across Europe and are known for high-energy, all-acoustic performances that turn any room into a dance floor.
